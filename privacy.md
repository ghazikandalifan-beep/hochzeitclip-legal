# Privacy Policy

**App:** hochzeitclip tool
**Last updated:** November 18, 2026

1. **Data we access.** With your permission via TikTok Login Kit, the app receives your basic TikTok profile info (open ID, display name, avatar) and an access token that allows it to upload videos to your account.
2. **How data is used.** This data is used only to upload clips that you have manually approved to your own TikTok account.
3. **Storage.** The access token and all videos are stored only locally on the owner's own computer. Nothing is stored on any external server operated by the app.
4. **Sharing.** No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or tracking.
5. **Deletion.** You can revoke the app's access at any time in your TikTok settings (Security and permissions, Apps). Local data can be deleted at any time.
6. **Changes.** This policy may be updated at any time on this page.
